# Scoring and action rules

Methodology version 1.0. If you change anything here, bump the version, copy the change into the repository's `methodology.md` with the date and the reason, and say in the next report that earlier scores used the older anchors.

## What gets scored

Score **developments** (a launch, a partnership, an acquisition, a pricing change), not companies. One development, one row, one score.

## Dimensions and weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Customer value and relevance | 30% |
| Competitive impact | 25% |
| Strategic relevance to MCF | 20% |
| Evidence of adoption or market traction | 15% |
| Urgency and time sensitivity | 10% |

Score each from 1 to 5, using whole numbers. The total is the weighted sum, to one decimal place, from 1.0 to 5.0. Write one line of reasoning per dimension. A score with no reasoning is not valid.

## Anchors

Use 2 and 4 for cases between the anchors.

**Customer value and relevance (30%)** — how much it helps a fleet operator do a real job
- 1: Cosmetic, or no clear operator job behind it.
- 3: Clearly improves a recognised job for a segment MCF serves.
- 5: Removes a major pain or enables a new job for MCF's core segments, and is likely to reset what customers expect.

**Competitive impact (25%)** — how much it changes the competitive picture
- 1: Parity with what the market already offers.
- 3: Strengthens one competitor in a segment that overlaps MCF.
- 5: Changes the competitive set or the buying criteria, for example a category shift or a major combination of rivals.

**Strategic relevance to MCF (20%)** — how close it is to what MCF is, as the baseline records it
- 1: Outside MCF's segments and propositions.
- 3: In a capability area MCF publicly competes in.
- 5: Directly meets MCF's documented differentiators or core platform (MyCF, the AI Assistant).

**Evidence of adoption or traction (15%)** — how real it is
- 1: Marketing claim only.
- 2: Announced, with no product evidence.
- 3: Demonstrated or generally available, no customer evidence.
- 4: Named customers or credible adoption signals.
- 5: Measured outcomes, or broad adoption shown by several independent sources.

**Urgency and time sensitivity (10%)** — how soon it matters
- 1: More than 18 months away, or no date.
- 3: Decision or market window within 6 to 12 months.
- 5: Available now and sold into MCF's segments, or a regulatory or market deadline within about 3 months.

## Bands

| Total | Band |
|---|---|
| 4.0 to 5.0 | Major |
| 3.0 to 3.9 | Notable |
| 2.0 to 2.9 | Minor |
| Below 2.0 | Noise |

A band is not an action. The action comes from the rules below.

## Confidence (separate from the score)

Never use confidence to raise or lower a score.

- **High:** a dated primary source and at least one independent corroboration.
- **Medium:** a dated primary source alone, or several reputable secondary sources that agree.
- **Low:** one secondary source, vendor marketing without product evidence, or mostly inference.

## Turning a score into an action

These are defaults. Use judgement and say when you depart from them.

- **Act:** total 3.5 or above, confidence at least Medium, and a named customer problem. Near-term action is justified by evidence already in hand.
- **Investigate:** total 3.0 or above with Low confidence, or a high score that rests on something that needs customer, technical or commercial validation.
- **Monitor:** a real signal with insufficient evidence or urgency. Name what would change the view.
- **No action:** low relevance or value. Log it and move on.

Limits that keep the report honest:
- A development with traction below 3 can't be **Act** on its own, however high it scores otherwise.
- At most three **Act** items per month unless the evidence is exceptional. If you have more, you are probably not prioritising.
- Every **Monitor** and **Investigate** has a trigger for reassessment.

## Consistency between months

- Before scoring, look at the last two months' top items and check the new scores against them. A development of similar weight should land in a similar place.
- If you change a score on an item from an earlier month, say why in the report.
- Rank by total. Break ties by evidence of adoption, then by urgency.

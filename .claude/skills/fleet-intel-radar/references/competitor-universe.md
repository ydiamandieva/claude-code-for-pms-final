# Competitor universe

Treat the names below as **candidates to validate**, not facts. Don't assume any of them is equally relevant, still independent, or still operating under the name listed. Corporate structures in this market change, so check current ownership from a primary source (company site, filings, newsroom) and date it.

## Candidates

**Direct and established**
Geotab, Samsara, Verizon Connect, Webfleet, Teletrac Navman, Powerfleet, Motive, AddSecure, Quartix, Fleet Complete (and its relationship to Powerfleet, if any: check).

**Adjacent and emerging**
Platform Science, Trimble Transportation, OEM-native fleet platforms, fleet-focused AI and analytics providers, emerging connected-mobility platforms, vertical SaaS companies expanding into telematics.

Add others you find during research, with the source that surfaced them.

## For each candidate, validate

- Whether it participates in fleet telematics today, and under what name and owner
- Geographic coverage, with the UK and Europe called out
- Customer segments and fleet types
- Overlap with MCF: same buyers, same use cases, same markets, or different
- Momentum: recent product activity, funding, partnerships, hiring signals

Date every check and record the source in the evidence register.

## Classes

1. **Direct competitor:** meaningful overlap in segments, geography and proposition.
2. **Segment-specific competitor:** competes with MCF in one segment, region or use case.
3. **Adjacent platform competitor:** a broader platform that touches MCF's space or could expand into it.
4. **Emerging or disruptive challenger:** small or new, but with a model or technology that could shift expectations.

## Choosing the monitoring group

- A **primary monitoring group** of about 8 to 12, plus a **watchlist** for the rest.
- Favour overlap with MCF's UK and European segments, scale and momentum, and AI activity. Include at least one adjacent platform and one emerging challenger so the group is not only incumbents.
- Write a sentence of justification for every inclusion and every notable exclusion.
- Don't include a company just because the brief named it, and don't drop one just because it is awkward.

## Quarterly review (January, April, July, October)

At the start of the monthly run in those months:
- Re-check ownership, mergers and exits.
- Promote or demote companies between the primary group and the watchlist, with reasons.
- Add newcomers that surfaced in the last three months.
- Record the review date and the changes in `competitors.md` and in the report.

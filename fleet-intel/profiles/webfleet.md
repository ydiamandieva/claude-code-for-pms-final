# Webfleet (Bridgestone): profile

**Class:** Direct · **Baseline v1.0 · last checked 2026-11-02** · Evidence IDs in `evidence-register.csv`.

- **Ownership and positioning:** Webfleet is Bridgestone's fleet management platform [F, E-0036]. Like MCF, it is a tyre maker's telematics business. Connected fleet management with driver terminals, vehicle cameras, tachograph and workflow management [E-0036].
- **Scale:** Berg Insight reportedly ranks it second largest in Europe by installed base [E-0047, Medium, second hand]. Frost & Sullivan named it European Commercial Fleet Telematics Company of the Year 2025, citing AI, EV and partner development [E-0038; analyst opinion].
- **AI:**
  - **Fleet Advisor** AI assistant: plain-language questions on fleet performance. A beta AI Assistant was shown at IAA Transportation 2024. Fleet Advisor was announced in May 2025, UK and Ireland first, with an international rollout later in 2025, and was integrated with OptiDrive 360 driver coaching [E-0034, E-0035]. A later trade report says it is available globally (not captured here). **Evidence-ladder level: 4** (generally available); no named customers or adoption figures found.
  - **Video 2.0:** AI-supported video telematics for accident investigation and claims, EU first [E-0037, Low: date not captured].
  - Showcased Fleet Advisor at RTX 2026 (30 June to 2 July) and planned an IAA Transportation 2026 showcase (15 to 20 September) of AI analytics and asset tracking [E-0036].
  - Bridgestone has talked of an AI-enabled "digital twin" for fleet managers within two years (older article, treat the timeline with caution).
- **Integrations:** OEM.connect programme, including a MAN Trucks integration that connects compatible vehicles without extra hardware [E-0036].
- **Research it sponsors:** a 1,800-manager study across 15 countries reports 15% of UK fleet managers already use AI, 33% plan to and 43% are considering it [E-0055, Low: vendor-sponsored].
- **Pricing:** not disclosed in the sources reviewed.
- **Strengths [I]:** the earliest generally available AI assistant among direct rivals researched; OEM data route; same tyre-maker angle as MCF. **Limitations and gaps:** adoption and outcome evidence absent; whether Fleet Advisor uses tyre data not checked; Video 2.0 dates unclear.

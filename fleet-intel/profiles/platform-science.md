# Platform Science (including Trimble's transportation telematics units): profile

**Class:** Adjacent platform competitor, and a channel partner of MCF · **Baseline v1.0 · last checked 2026-11-02** · Evidence IDs in `evidence-register.csv`.

- **Structure:** Platform Science bought Trimble's global transportation telematics business units, closing on 2025-02-08. Trimble holds a 32.5% stake and a board seat [F, E-0046, E-0066]. Trimble's remaining transportation software was not assessed.
- **Proposition:** Virtual Vehicle, an application platform that runs at the edge on factory-installed truck hardware, built with truck makers (the 2024 release names Daimler Truck North America, Navistar and PACCAR) [E-0067, Medium]. It works as an open ecosystem and marketplace for apps and services.
- **Relationship to MCF [F]:** MCF's Smart Predictive Tire is listed on the Virtual Vehicle Marketplace (announced 2026-07-23), and the MCF and Doran tyre monitoring integration is hosted there (2026-09-02) [E-0006, E-0007]. [I] This gives MCF an OEM-embedded route to market, and it also puts a competitor's platform between MCF and some fleets.
- **AI:** no 2026 agent or assistant announcement found in a single search (searched once; not conclusive).
- **Unverified 2026 items:** Canadian ELD certification for DTNA hardware (May 2026) and the end of GEOTrac and Trimble Oil & Gas services (7 February 2026) appear only on an aggregator [E-0061, Low].
- **Geography note:** the evidence is mostly North American, so overlap with UK and European fleets is unclear.
- **Pricing:** not disclosed in the sources reviewed.
- **Gaps:** European customer base; whether Virtual Vehicle is sold in the UK and EU; AI plans.

# Geotab: profile

**Class:** Direct · **Baseline v1.0 · last checked 2026-11-02** · Evidence IDs in `evidence-register.csv`.

- **Proposition and customers:** fleet telematics platform (MyGeotab) sold through its own teams and resellers. Company figures: 100,000+ customers, 6 million+ connected vehicles and assets, 100 billion+ data points a day, 1 million+ active subscriptions in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, 13 EMEA locations and two R&D centres [company claims, E-0016].
- **Geography and segments:** global. UK and Europe strengthened on 2025-10-01 by acquiring Verizon Connect's commercial operations in Australia and eight European countries (UK, Ireland, Italy, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal), with 400+ staff joining. Geotab says it is focusing on small and mid-sized fleets. Product and engineering teams were excluded [F, E-0014, E-0015].
- **Platform capabilities (verified):** new GO and GO Plus devices (satellite tracking, Wi-Fi hotspot, accident data buffer) and GO Anywhere trailer and asset tracking, all feeding MyGeotab. Both lines are due in Europe by end of 2026 (announced or planned in Europe) [E-0016]. GO Focus Pro dash cam announced for North America in Q2 2026 [E-0017].
- **AI:** Ace generative AI assistant, unveiled February 2024 [E-0018], early adopter trials in 2024, rolled out to Southeast Asia and Australia in October 2025, and described as generally available to all customers at Geotab Connect 2026 (multilingual, voice agent) [E-0017, Medium]. An MCP connector for external AI models was planned for end of Q1 2026; a June 2026 "AI Connector for Fleets" headline was seen but not confirmed [E-0017]. **Evidence-ladder level: 4** (generally available). Customer outcomes: early adopter quotes exist but were not verified.
- **Partnerships and integrations:** not researched beyond the Verizon Connect transaction.
- **Pricing:** not disclosed in the sources reviewed.
- **Recent events:** Verizon Connect acquisition (2025-10-01); Geotab Connect Europe, Barcelona (2026-05-19); Geotab Connect 2026, Las Vegas (date not captured).
- **Strengths [I]:** scale in EMEA, an AI assistant publicly unveiled more than two years before MCF's launch, a hardware refresh, and new UK and EU sales capacity from Verizon Connect.
- **Limitations and gaps:** integration of acquired customers is unproven; Ace adoption data and pricing not public; ownership not checked; Ace languages and UK specifics not verified.

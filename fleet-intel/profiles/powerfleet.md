# Powerfleet (including Fleet Complete): profile

**Class:** Direct · **Baseline v1.0 · last checked 2026-11-02** · Evidence IDs in `evidence-register.csv`.

- **Structure:** Powerfleet acquired Fleet Complete in October 2024. Fleet Complete is moving to the Powerfleet brand, with existing contracts and integrations unchanged. The reported deal size is about $200 million with about 600,000 subscribers added [F: acquisition, E-0031, E-0032; reported figures, E-0068]. Powerfleet's platform is Unity; Fleet Complete's FC Hub and AI video product FC Vision are being aligned with it [E-0031].
- **Financials (company):** fiscal 2026 (year to 2026-03-31) revenue $443.8 million (+22%), adjusted EBITDA $97.0 million (+44%), AI video bookings up more than 50% [E-0033, Medium].
- **AI:** AI video and safety (Unity VisionAI, Safety-as-a-Service) is the visible AI line, with bookings growth as an adoption signal. **Evidence-ladder level for AI video: 4 to 5** (generally available, bookings growth; outcomes not seen). No conversational assistant or agentic product found in a search focused on 2026 (searched).
- **Europe:** earnings material refers to channel partnerships signed in North America and Europe; a single aggregator says Accenture selected Powerfleet as a safety partner in Central Europe in May 2026 [E-0070, Low, not relied on].
- **Conflict:** aggregator pages dated July 2026 describe a "$200M acquisition" as new; the figures match the 2024 deal, so they are treated as mis-dated and not used [E-0062].
- **Not verified in this pass:** Q1 fiscal 2027 results (August 2026) and a reported guidance change and new chief AI officer, which came only from aggregators.
- **Pricing:** not disclosed in the sources reviewed.
- **Strengths [I]:** scale after the combination, AI video growth. **Limitations and gaps:** integration of Unity and FC Hub; no assistant evidence; European share unknown.

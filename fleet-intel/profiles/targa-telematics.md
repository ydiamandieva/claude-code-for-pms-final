# Targa Telematics: profile

**Class:** Direct in Europe (strong in rental and leasing) · **Baseline v1.0 · last checked 2026-11-02** · Evidence IDs in `evidence-register.csv`.

- **Scale (company citing Berg Insight):** largest European fleet management vendor by active installed base, about 900,000 units at the end of 2024, for the third year running [E-0044, Medium]. Also reported as the largest in rental and leasing, with 979,000 systems at end 2025 [E-0045, Low]. **Conflict:** an Italian outlet reports a 7.8% share and 2.13 million connected vehicles in 2026, which does not match the 900,000 figure and could not be confirmed [Low, not used for sizing].
- **AI:** says it is integrating agentic AI models so AI supports decisions as well as analysing data (announced) [E-0045]. A company-sourced claim says agentic AI tools cut UK fleet vehicle thefts by 12% in 2025 [Low, unverified]. With Politecnico di Milano it reported that 73% of Italian fleet managers are evaluating AI [E-0057, vendor-sponsored].
- **Evidence-ladder level:** 2 (announced). Whether anything is generally available was not verified.
- **Business:** a 2025 results and international growth press release exists [E-0043, title only, not opened].
- **Pricing:** not disclosed in the sources reviewed.
- **Why it matters [I]:** the biggest named installed base in Europe, and UK activity is implied by the theft claim; useful as a benchmark for how a European-scale vendor frames AI. **Gaps:** market-share figures conflict; product detail and UK footprint not researched.

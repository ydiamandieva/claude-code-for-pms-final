# Michelin Connected Fleet (MCF): profile

**Baseline v1.0 · last checked 2026-11-02.** Method is the same as for competitors. Where nothing public was found, the label is *not publicly verified*, which is not a statement that a capability is missing. Evidence IDs point to `evidence-register.csv`.

## Positioning and scale

- Michelin presents MCF as "a fully-fledged partner" rather than a tyre supplier, combining AI, Michelin's own algorithms and knowledge of how vehicles are used [F, E-0003, page updated 2026-07-21].
- Company figures: 70,000 customers, 48 countries, data from about 300 million journeys a year [F, company claim, E-0003]. A June 2026 release is reported to cite 660,000 connected vehicles [E-0004, Medium: summary only, page not opened].
- **Conflict:** a third-party review gives more than 50,000 customers and 684,000 connected assets in 20 countries [E-0005]. Treated as less reliable than the company's own figures, not as proof of either.
- Origins: the brand brought together Masternaut in Europe, NexTraq in North America and Sascar in South America [E-0065, 2022]. Launched in Spain in October 2020 [E-0003].
- UK customers named for the AI Assistant: commercial vehicle and passenger transport [E-0001].

## AI

- **AI Assistant** inside MyConnectedFleet, announced **2026-06-29**. Users type questions in natural language and get text or visual answers, reports and trends from real-time data. Example questions: most fuel-efficient drivers this month, which vehicles need tyre maintenance, what fuel cost this month [F, E-0001].
- Stated as "currently available" in ten countries: UK, US, France, Spain, Denmark, the Netherlands, Finland, Sweden, Germany and Italy. Described as a "closed system" for data confidentiality. Co-developed with some of Michelin's largest customers (company claim). Features to be added gradually on customer feedback [E-0001, E-0002].
- **Evidence-ladder level: 4, generally available (company statement).** No pricing, supported languages, adoption data or measured outcomes were found. The release's "78% of fleet managers believe AI will transform the sector" is a Michelin survey of 400+ operators in France and the UK (March 2025). It measures opinion, not product results [E-0001].
- **Progression:** descriptive plus natural-language querying is verified. Predictive tyre intelligence is verified (company claim). Recommended actions and automated execution are *not publicly verified*.

## Ecosystem and partnerships

- Platform Science: Smart Predictive Tire listed on the Virtual Vehicle Marketplace, announced 2026-07-23 [E-0006].
- Doran: tyre pressure monitoring hardware integrated with Smart Predictive Tire, announced 2026-09-02 [E-0007, E-0008].
- Solution partners (Krone, Schmitz Cargobull, TACHOfresh, VisionTrack) and sales partners [E-0011, E-0012].

## Evidence of differentiation

- [F] Michelin is a tyre maker and the data and algorithms it cites are tyre-related. Webfleet is owned by Bridgestone, another tyre maker.
- [I] Tyre condition and predictive tyre maintenance are the clearest differentiated area, because they are tied to Michelin's own tyre knowledge and are supported by a hardware partner (Doran) and a marketplace channel.
- [H] Grounding the AI Assistant in tyre data could be a differentiator against assistants that rely on general vehicle data. Not tested.

## Strengths, limitations, evidence gaps

- **Strengths [I]:** tyre intelligence; geographic reach; brand; the Assistant launched in ten countries at once; new channels via Platform Science.
- **Limitations [I]:** the Assistant arrived about 13 months after Webfleet's Fleet Advisor and after Geotab's Ace began trials in 2024; public documentation of the platform is thin (E-0009 covers four components only). One 2026 third-party review says the platform lacks advanced AI dashcam and driver coaching features (E-0005, secondary, treat with caution).
- **Not publicly verified (searched, nothing found):** pricing; AI Assistant languages, adoption and outcomes; voice interaction; third-party AI connectivity (for example MCP); agentic or automated actions.
- **Not publicly verified (not searched this pass):** depth of AI video; driver coaching detail; routing and dispatch; workflow automation; OEM data integration; API catalogue; security and privacy certifications; release notes for 2025 and 2026.

# Market baseline and strategic baseline

**Baseline v1.0 · research cut-off 2026-11-02 · UK and Europe first.** Tags: [F] fact, [I] inference, [H] hypothesis. Evidence IDs in `evidence-register.csv`. Figures from third parties are marked as estimates.

## A. Market overview

**Structure and segments**
- [I] The market is crowded and fragmented, with a few very large global platforms, regional specialists, OEM-embedded platforms and a long tail of mid-size European vendors.
- Segments visible in the evidence: small and mid-sized fleets (Geotab's stated focus after Verizon Connect; Quartix's UK base) [E-0014, E-0039]; large and enterprise fleets (Samsara: 61% of ARR from customers above $100,000) [E-0019]; OEM-embedded and truck-maker portals (Platform Science, Volvo Connect, Mercedes-Benz Fleetboard) [E-0067, E-0048, E-0049]; rental and leasing (Targa Telematics) [E-0045]; tyre-maker-owned platforms (Webfleet under Bridgestone, MCF under Michelin) [E-0036, E-0003].

**Size and concentration (third-party estimates, not verified)**
- Berg Insight, as summarised by ResearchAndMarkets: installed base of fleet management systems in Europe to reach 30.5 million by 2029 [E-0047]. About 18 million at end 2024 comes from a report author's LinkedIn post (Low). Mordor Intelligence estimates 28.16 million installed units in Europe for 2026, but covers a different segment [E-0045, Low].
- Targa Telematics is reported as the largest European vendor with about 900,000 units at end 2024 [E-0044]. [I] If the 18 million figure holds, the largest vendor holds a single-digit share of the installed base, which points to a fragmented market. This mixes sources of Medium and Low confidence.
- **Note on comparability:** Geotab reports 1 million+ active subscriptions in EMEA [E-0016]. That is a company claim about a different region, unit and date than Berg Insight's Europe installed base, so the two cannot be ranked against each other.
- MCF reports about 660,000 vehicles and 70,000 customers across 48 countries and three continents [E-0003, E-0004]. No European-only figure was found.

**Technology and product trends**
- Conversational AI assistants are now generally available at several vendors: Webfleet (May 2025), Geotab Ace (2026), MCF (June 2026), with Motive announced for the UK [E-0034, E-0017, E-0001, E-0025].
- Early "agent" language: Samsara's AI Safety Coach and planned agents, Motive Automations, Targa's agentic AI, and external AI connectivity through MCP at Geotab (planned) and Motive (reported) [E-0019, E-0025, E-0045, E-0017, E-0026].
- AI video and edge hardware: Motive AI Dashcam Plus, Geotab GO Focus Pro, Powerfleet AI video bookings up 50%+, Webfleet Video 2.0, and Quartix planning a dashcam for Continental Europe in 2027 [E-0024, E-0017, E-0033, E-0037, E-0040].
- Hardware-free and OEM routes to vehicle data: Webfleet OEM.connect with MAN, Platform Science on factory-installed hardware, truck-maker portals adding AI insights [E-0036, E-0067, E-0048, E-0049].
- Predictive tyre and maintenance intelligence, led by MCF in this evidence set (other vendors were not searched) [E-0003, E-0006, E-0007].
- Asset and trailer tracking: Geotab GO Anywhere (Europe by end 2026), MCF trailer data with Krone and Schmitz Cargobull [E-0016, E-0011].

**Customer needs and purchasing criteria**
- Evidence on adoption is vendor-sponsored: a Webfleet study of 1,800 managers across 15 countries reports 15% of UK fleet managers use AI, 33% plan to and 43% are considering it [E-0055, Low]. A Targa and Politecnico di Milano study reports 73% of Italian managers evaluating AI [E-0057, Low]. A Geotab study from October 2024 found UK managers more cautious than European peers [E-0056, Low, dated]. Michelin's own March 2025 survey of 400+ operators in France and the UK reports 78% believe AI will transform the sector [E-0001; opinion, not outcomes].
- Dashcam adoption in continental Europe is flagged as constrained by GDPR and driver consent [E-0069, Low].
- **Not found:** a 2026 survey of purchasing criteria for UK and European fleets (price, integration, data privacy, support). This is an open gap.

**Regulation and ecosystem**
- From 2026-07-01, light commercial vehicles over 2.5 tonnes used in international transport or cabotage must have second-generation smart tachographs, and some UK operators carrying goods between the UK and EU follow the drivers' hours and tachograph rules. Sources differ on the exact weight band; a DVSA correction in May narrowed the UK scope. Fleet News reports 88% of affected fleets still needing a unit and 27% of operators confident they are ready (Low) [E-0050, E-0051, E-0052].
- The EU Data Act has applied since 2025-09-12: owners and fleet operators are entitled to their vehicle data at no cost and may nominate third parties; Commission guidance says it has no rules on access to vehicle functions or resources [E-0053, E-0054; advocacy framing in the first].

**Consolidation and partnerships (dated events)**
- 2024-06: AddSecure takes Astrata Europe's telematics division [E-0042].
- 2024-10: Powerfleet acquires Fleet Complete [E-0031].
- 2025-02-08: Platform Science closes purchase of Trimble's transportation telematics units [E-0046, E-0066].
- 2025-10-01: Geotab acquires Verizon Connect's commercial operations in Australia and eight European countries [E-0014].
- 2026-05-28: AddSecure signs to take over Spotmaster's fleet connections [E-0041].
- 2026-07-06: Respida Capital completes its majority purchase of Teletrac Navman [E-0028].
- 2026-07-23 and 2026-09-02: MCF and Platform Science, and MCF and Doran (partnerships) [E-0006, E-0007].
- Berg Insight counts eleven vendor mergers and acquisitions in Europe in the year reported (year not captured) [E-0047].

**Disruption signals**
- Motive: a funded, hardware-plus-AI entrant in the UK [E-0023, E-0024, E-0027].
- AI-native startups (Nomad, Proaction, Carma) are small and mostly North American; low relevance today [E-0058, E-0059, E-0060].
- [H] If assistants can be reached by outside AI models through connectors such as MCP, a customer's own AI tools could query several vendors' fleet data, which would reduce the value of each vendor's own assistant interface. Only announced or reported so far.

## E. Strategic baseline

**Competitive positioning summary**
- [I] MCF is a scaled, multi-region telematics provider with a clear differentiator in tyre intelligence and an AI Assistant that went live in ten countries at once in June 2026. It is later to market in conversational AI than Webfleet (May 2025) and Geotab (unveiled February 2024), and its public documentation of platform capabilities is thin.
- [I] Both tyre makers (Michelin, Bridgestone) run telematics platforms. Webfleet is the nearest like-for-like rival on the "AI plus tyres" story, so this comparison deserves the most scrutiny.
- The competitor set is shifting at the UK and European edges: Geotab absorbing Verizon Connect's UK and EU commercial operations, Motive entering the UK, Teletrac Navman changing owner, and AddSecure and Powerfleet consolidating.

**Market maturity**
- Core telematics (tracking, driver behaviour, compliance) is mature and consolidating [I].
- AI assistants are moving from announced to generally available, but adoption and measured outcomes are not public. The radar classes them as *Experimenting* until adoption evidence appears.
- Agentic execution is *Emerging*. Pricing for agents is not settled (Samsara said so explicitly) [E-0019].

**Capability gaps and differentiation opportunities** (hypotheses, not findings)
- [H] Ground the AI Assistant in tyre and predictive-maintenance data to answer questions no general-purpose assistant can.
- [H] Public evidence of what the Assistant actually does for customers (use cases, adoption, measured outcomes) is absent for every vendor; the first to publish credible outcomes could set the benchmark.
- [H] MCF's platform documentation and release notes are sparse in public; the thin record makes it hard for buyers and analysts to verify capabilities (an evidence gap for MCF).

**Emerging technology adoption patterns**
- Assistants first, then voice, then agents; most vendors are between "announced" and "generally available". Evidence of realised value is missing across the group.

**Strategic threats and opportunities**
- Threats: Geotab's larger UK and EU sales capacity [E-0014]; Motive's AI-plus-hardware push in the UK [E-0024]; assistants becoming a baseline expectation after 13+ months of availability elsewhere [E-0034]; OEM-embedded data routes lowering the need for aftermarket hardware [E-0036, E-0067].
- Opportunities: customers of Verizon Connect Reveal in the UK and EU, who may be considering their options [E-0015]; the July 2026 tachograph change for vans [E-0050]; tyre intelligence through partners and marketplaces [E-0006, E-0007].

**Key uncertainties**
1. Real adoption and outcomes for any vendor's assistant.
2. Whether Samsara's agents are generally available in the UK and Europe, and at what price.
3. What MCF's Assistant can do beyond the examples in the release (depth, languages, actions).
4. The Verizon Connect Reveal support window for UK and EU customers (one source says up to 18 months).
5. Teletrac Navman's direction under its new owner.
6. Purchasing criteria of UK and European fleets in 2026.
7. How MCF compares on AI video, coaching, routing and compliance, which were not searched in depth.

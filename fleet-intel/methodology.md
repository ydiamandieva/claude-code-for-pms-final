# Methodology: version 1.0 (2026-11-02)

This is the copy of the scoring and taxonomy definitions used for the v1.0 baseline. If anything below changes, bump the version, record the change and date in the log at the end, and say in the next report that earlier scores used the older definitions.

## Scoring

Score **developments**, not companies. Whole numbers 1 to 5 on five weighted dimensions. Total = weighted sum, one decimal, 1.0 to 5.0. One line of reasoning per dimension. Scores are judgement, not market facts.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Customer value and relevance | 30% |
| Competitive impact | 25% |
| Strategic relevance to MCF | 20% |
| Evidence of adoption or market traction | 15% |
| Urgency and time sensitivity | 10% |

**Anchors** (use 2 and 4 in between)
- **Customer value (30%):** 1 cosmetic or no clear operator job. 3 clearly improves a recognised job for a segment MCF serves. 5 removes a major pain or enables a new job for MCF's core segments and is likely to reset customer expectations.
- **Competitive impact (25%):** 1 parity with what the market offers. 3 strengthens one competitor in a segment overlapping MCF. 5 changes the competitive set or the buying criteria.
- **Strategic relevance to MCF (20%):** 1 outside MCF's segments and propositions. 3 in a capability area MCF publicly competes in. 5 directly meets MCF's documented differentiators or core platform (MyCF, the AI Assistant).
- **Evidence of adoption or traction (15%):** 1 marketing claim only. 2 announced with no product evidence. 3 demonstrated or generally available, no customer evidence. 4 named customers or credible adoption signals. 5 measured outcomes or broad adoption across several independent sources.
- **Urgency (10%):** 1 more than 18 months away or undated. 3 window or decision in 6 to 12 months. 5 available now and sold into MCF's segments, or a regulatory or market deadline within about 3 months.

**Bands:** 4.0 to 5.0 Major · 3.0 to 3.9 Notable · 2.0 to 2.9 Minor · below 2.0 Noise. A band is not an action.

**Confidence** (separate from the score; never used to raise or lower it): High = a dated primary source plus an independent corroboration. Medium = a dated primary source alone, or several reputable secondary sources that agree. Low = one secondary source, vendor marketing without product evidence, or mostly inference.

**Actions (defaults; say when departing from them)**
- **Act:** total 3.5 or above, confidence at least Medium, a named customer problem.
- **Investigate:** total 3.0 or above with Low confidence, or a high score that needs customer, technical or commercial validation.
- **Monitor:** a real signal with insufficient evidence or urgency. Name what would change the view.
- **No action:** low relevance or value. Log and move on.
- A development with traction below 3 cannot be Act on its own. At most three Act items a month unless the evidence is exceptional. Every Monitor and Investigate has a trigger for reassessment.
- Tie-break: higher evidence of adoption, then higher urgency, then higher confidence.

## Taxonomy

Domains: 1 Tracking and core telematics · 2 Driver safety and behaviour · 3 Video and computer vision · 4 Maintenance and diagnostics · 5 Fuel, EV and sustainability · 6 Compliance and regulatory · 7 Operations and workflow · 8 Analytics and intelligence · 9 AI assistants and automation · 10 Data, integrations and ecosystem · 11 Commercial and platform · 12 Segment fit.

**Status labels:** Publicly verified · Partially verified · Announced or planned · Not publicly verified (never means "missing") · Not applicable. **Relative position:** Leading · Competitive · Lagging · Unclear.

**AI evidence ladder:** 1 marketing claim · 2 announced · 3 demonstrated · 4 generally available · 5 adoption with realised value. **AI progression:** descriptive → predictive → recommended actions → automated execution.

**Radar levels:** Emerging (early signals, few vendors, mostly announced or demonstrated) · Experimenting (several vendors live in pilots, previews or limited release) · Scaling (generally available at multiple vendors with visible customer adoption) · Mainstream (expected by customers and offered by most of the primary group).

## Conventions used in this repository

- Tags: [F] fact, [I] inference, [H] hypothesis, [R] recommendation.
- NPV *(searched)* means a search found nothing. NPV *(not searched)* means the capability was not searched for in that pass.
- Baseline scoring covers the thirteen most consequential developments found, including some older than the month covered, because they define the starting position.

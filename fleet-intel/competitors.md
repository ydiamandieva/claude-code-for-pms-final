## Candidates from the brief that were not placed in the group

- **Fleet Complete:** treated as part of Powerfleet (see above). Not a separate independent competitor.
- **Trimble Transportation:** its global transportation telematics units moved to Platform Science, so it is covered under Platform Science. What remains inside Trimble was not assessed.
- **OEM-native, AI and analytics providers, vertical SaaS:** only partially assessed (see watchlist). The OEM-native category is a real gap in this baseline.

## Limitations of this validation

- Ownership was checked from trade media and company pages, not from filings or registries, except where noted.
- Geotab's own ownership structure was not checked.
- Scania, Lytx, Frotcom, Mapon and similar vendors were not researched.
- The monitoring group favours vendors that publish AI news. Quiet incumbents with large installed bases may be under-represented.

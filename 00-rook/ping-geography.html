Share of callouts nobody took, by week

Week starting. The 10 Aug week mixes both periods. Gray = before 4.2, red = from the 10 Aug week on.

Nobody took 48 of 879 callouts before 4.2 (5.5%) and 49 of 440 after (11.1%). Observed. Two things moved. First, far more first pings failed: 27% before, 42% after, mostly because the share missed went from 3% to 21%. Second, a failed first ping was less often rescued: 20% ended with nobody before, 27% after. By simple arithmetic on these counts, about half of the doubling comes from each. These are small numbers (48 and 49 callouts), so treat the split as a rough guide.

One thing the data cannot explain: 42 of the 48 untaken callouts before, and 40 of the 49 after, had only one ping sent. The code walks down the whole list of available responders, so a one-ping ending most likely means only one responder was available. That needs the staff engineer to confirm, and it points at availability as much as at ranking.
